\begin{resumo}[Abstract]
 \begin{otherlanguage*}{english}
This paper aims to develop a System for Internship Hiring Management (SIGCE), 
focused on centralizing and optimizing the internship signing and hiring process at the 
Faculty of Science and Engineering Technologies (FCTE). The proposed system seeks to integrate 
and automate the stages involved in internship management, improving efficiency, accessibility, 
and transparency of information while reducing administrative burden. 
Currently, the internship hiring and signing process at FCTE is decentralized 
and carried out manually, leading to issues such as data redundancy, inconsistent 
information, and difficulty in tracking activities. The SIGCE system addresses these 
problems by centralizing information, enhancing communication between students, professors, 
supervisors, and administrative sectors, and ensuring better control over deadlines and required 
documents, such as internship agreements. 
The development of SIGCE follows an agile methodology, using Scrum to ensure incremental deliveries 
and greater flexibility. The system's architecture is based on the principles of Clean Architecture 
and Domain-Driven Design (DDD), with the implementation of microservices to ensure scalability and 
flexibility. Furthermore, the system will integrate technologies such as the Federal Government’s Single 
Sign-On and digital signatures, ensuring higher security and compliance with legal standards. 
In the end, SIGCE is expected to offer a robust solution for internship hiring management at FCTE, 
promoting a faster and more transparent experience for all stakeholders.

   \noindent 
   \textbf{Key-words}: digital signature. internships. development. software.
 \end{otherlanguage*}
\end{resumo}
