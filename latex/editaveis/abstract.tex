\begin{resumo}[Abstract]
 \begin{otherlanguage*}{english}
This work presents SIGCE, a web system for internship hiring at the Faculty of Sciences and Technologies in Engineering (FCTE), University of Brasília. Between the first and the second stages of the project, SIGAA began handling the internship process with Gov.br signatures, so the contribution is no longer a channel that the university lacked. SIGCE aims at an easier path than that institutional module for the Software Engineering commission. Advisor assignment is automatic, using the radius rule already applied by the commission's Telegram bot, and the agreement uploaded by the student is checked by a generative model, which reports pending items and does not replace the professor's review. Development was applied, with requirements elicited in a brainstorming session. The application has a Next.js frontend, a Spring Boot backend, and PostgreSQL. Students and professors sign in through Login Único. The student, the company supervisor, and the professor sign the agreement through the Gov.br electronic-signature API.

   \vspace{\onelineskip}
 
   \noindent 
   \textbf{Key-words}: internship management. advisor assignment. document review. SIGAA.
 \end{otherlanguage*}
\end{resumo}
